**How a recall quiz works:** 5 questions, asked one at a time in chat at the start of S1. Four come from the previous day's section of the bank and one is a *spaced* question from any earlier day (weak spots in PROGRESS.md come first). Closed book, about 10 minutes, 0.2 points each.

**How a stage test works:** 45 minutes, closed book, timed from the system clock. Concept sections are answered in chat. Hands-on sections are done in your Snowflake account and saved to `work/day-NN/stage-test.sql`. You need ≥ 70% to move on; below that, a remediation session and a retake built from different questions.

**Release policy:** a recall bank is released once its last day has been quizzed (for example, `recall-stage-1` after Day 6 · S1). A stage test is released right after it's graded. Your results are then added at the bottom of the file.

**Honor rule:** don't open sealed files in an editor. The banks are bigger than any one sitting, and I rephrase and add questions, so peeking only costs you the practice.
